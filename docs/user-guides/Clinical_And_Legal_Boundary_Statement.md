# Clinical And Legal Boundary Statement

Status: mandatory boundary statement for governed Panacea OS validation and deployment review
Scope: Panacea OS v4.0 enterprise validation environments

## Required Boundary

Clinical and legal approval required before real clinical production use.

Protected sample records should be used unless documented legal, privacy, regulatory, security, operational, and clinical governance approval authorizes live organizational data.

Panacea OS does not replace clinicians.

Panacea OS does not autonomously diagnose patients.

Panacea OS does not autonomously prescribe treatment.

Human approval is required for clinical decisions.

Guided validation must be controlled, documented, monitored, and reversible.

## Data Boundary

Enterprise validation must use protected sample records unless all required approvals are granted.

Do not enter, import, upload, process, or expose live patient records in a validation environment without written approval from the responsible operator, privacy owner, legal owner, security owner, and clinical governance owner.

## Clinical Safety Boundary

Any clinical-facing recommendation, summary, review, alert, or workflow output must remain advisory unless a qualified human user reviews and approves it according to local policy.

No Panacea OS validation or deployment review environment may be used to make autonomous clinical decisions.

## Legal And Regulatory Boundary

The operator is responsible for confirming local laws, data protection rules, healthcare regulations, patient consent requirements, cybersecurity requirements, and professional governance requirements before any use involving real organizations, clinicians, patients, or data.

## Governance Review Boundary

Every governed validation environment must document:

- environment owner,
- deployment scope,
- approved users,
- data classification,
- permitted workflows,
- prohibited workflows,
- backup and rollback plan,
- incident escalation path,
- audit evidence location,
- final acceptance decision.

## Final Statement

Ready for governed enterprise validation on a server only when this boundary is accepted and documented.

Not approved for real clinical production use.
