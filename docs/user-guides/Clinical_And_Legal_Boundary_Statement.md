# Clinical And Legal Boundary Statement

Status: mandatory boundary statement for controlled Panacea OS pilot use
Scope: Panacea OS v4.0 external pilot environments

## Required Boundary

Panacea OS is not approved for real clinical production use.

No real patient data should be used without documented legal, privacy, regulatory, security, operational, and clinical governance approval.

Panacea OS does not replace clinicians.

Panacea OS does not autonomously diagnose patients.

Panacea OS does not autonomously prescribe treatment.

Human approval is required for clinical decisions.

Pilot use must be controlled, documented, monitored, and reversible.

## Data Boundary

Controlled pilot validation must use approved non-production data unless all required approvals are granted.

Do not enter, import, upload, process, or expose real patient data in a pilot environment without written approval from the responsible operator, privacy owner, legal owner, security owner, and clinical governance owner.

## Clinical Safety Boundary

Any clinical-facing recommendation, summary, review, alert, or workflow output must remain advisory unless a qualified human user reviews and approves it according to local policy.

No Panacea OS pilot deployment may be used to make autonomous clinical decisions.

## Legal And Regulatory Boundary

The operator is responsible for confirming local laws, data protection rules, healthcare regulations, patient consent requirements, cybersecurity requirements, and professional governance requirements before any use involving real organizations, clinicians, patients, or data.

## Pilot Governance Boundary

Every controlled pilot must document:

- pilot owner,
- deployment scope,
- approved users,
- data classification,
- permitted workflows,
- prohibited workflows,
- backup and rollback plan,
- incident escalation path,
- audit evidence location,
- final acceptance decision.

## Final Statement

Ready to execute real external controlled pilot deployment on a server only when this boundary is accepted and documented.

Not approved for real clinical production use.
