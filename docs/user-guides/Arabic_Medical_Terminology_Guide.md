## Safety Labels

| English | Arabic |
|---|---|
| Advisory only. Clinician remains final decision maker. | للاسترشاد فقط. يبقى القرار النهائي للطبيب المختص. |
| DEMO DATA — NOT REAL PATIENT DATA | بيانات تجريبية — ليست بيانات مرضى حقيقية |

## Terminology Rules

- Use clear hospital Arabic rather than informal phrases.
- Keep clinical safety labels direct and unambiguous.
- Keep patient-facing wording simpler than clinician-facing wording.
- Keep codes, API paths, and medical standard identifiers in English/LTR.
