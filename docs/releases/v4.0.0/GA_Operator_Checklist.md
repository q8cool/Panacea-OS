# GA Operator Checklist

- Confirm approved release commit.
- Confirm all final quality gates pass.
- Confirm secret scan output.
- Confirm remote CI evidence or accepted waiver.
- Confirm live Foundation provider verification.
- Confirm database migration execution.
- Confirm backup and restore drill.
- Confirm Docker image build evidence.
- Confirm Kubernetes deployment evidence.
- Confirm security and privacy policy configuration.
- Confirm clinical and AI governance hold points.
- Create GA tag only after approval.
