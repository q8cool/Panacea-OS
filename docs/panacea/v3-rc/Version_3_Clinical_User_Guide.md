# Version 3 Clinical User Guide

## Clinical Boundary

Version 3 RC supports governed healthcare operations, privacy, AI assurance, compliance, workforce, legal, product, and customer success workflows. It does not replace clinicians and does not perform autonomous diagnosis or treatment.

## Clinical Safety Expectations

Clinical users retain final responsibility for clinical decisions. AI, privacy, compliance, and operational signals are advisory, auditable, and subject to policy controls.

## Validated Clinical Safety Areas

- Clinical traceability
- Clinical recommendation auditability
- Medication safety integration
- Allergy safety integration
- Patient safety workflows
- Clinical governance workflows
- Sentinel event workflow
- Infection control workflow
- Emergency, ICU, and surgery workflow coverage in the release validation matrix

## Escalation

Any clinical safety concern must be recorded through the appropriate patient safety, audit, compliance, or AI incident workflow.
