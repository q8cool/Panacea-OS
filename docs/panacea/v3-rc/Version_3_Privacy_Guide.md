# Version 3 Privacy Guide

## Privacy Position

Version 3 RC includes the Global Enterprise Data Privacy, Consent and Trust Platform. Privacy and consent workflows are governed, auditable, and policy-controlled.

## Required Controls

- Consent enforcement
- Purpose-based access control
- Data minimization
- Retention policy enforcement
- Data residency controls
- Cross-border policy enforcement
- Tenant isolation
- Audit integrity
- Trust relationship verification

## Data Sharing Boundary

No data may be shared, processed, or exposed outside policy boundaries. Cross-border sharing requires policy, consent, trust, residency, and approval evidence.

## Events

Privacy events include consent lifecycle, data access, data export, privacy policy updates, data sharing approval, privacy violation detection, and trust relationship lifecycle events.
