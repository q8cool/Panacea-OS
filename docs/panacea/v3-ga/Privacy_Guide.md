# Version 3.0 Privacy Guide

## Privacy Baseline

Version 3.0 GA includes governed privacy, consent, data rights, data sharing, trust, and monitoring workflows.

## Required Controls

- Consent enforcement.
- Purpose-based access control.
- Data minimization.
- Retention policy enforcement.
- Data residency validation.
- Cross-border privacy validation.
- Tenant isolation.
- Audit trail validation.

## Production Requirements

No data may be shared, processed, or exposed outside approved policy boundaries. Cross-border data flows require policy, consent, trust, residency, and approval evidence.
