# Version 3.0 Global Readiness Guide

## Scope

Global readiness covers multi-country, multi-region, data residency, data sovereignty, localization, currency, time zone, cross-border federation, consent policy, and privacy policy validation.

## Required Evidence

- Country and jurisdiction configuration.
- Approved countries in governance contexts.
- Cross-border data sharing approvals.
- Tenant isolation validation.
- Consent and privacy policy enforcement evidence.

## GA Position

Version 3.0 GA is globally ready for controlled production rollout after site-specific acceptance evidence is attached.
