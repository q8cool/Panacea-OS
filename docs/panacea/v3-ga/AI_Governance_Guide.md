# Version 3.0 AI Governance Guide

## AI Governance Baseline

Version 3.0 GA validates AI assurance, model risk, prompt approval, agent approval, AI safety testing, AI monitoring, AI incident management, and regulatory AI governance.

## Production Promotion Rule

No AI model, prompt, tool, agent, recommendation engine, or automation workflow may be promoted to production without authorized approval.

## Required Controls

- Human approval workflow.
- Recommendation traceability.
- Model validation evidence.
- Prompt and agent approval evidence.
- AI incident workflow.
- Bias, drift, hallucination, unsafe output, and performance monitoring.

## Safety Boundary

No autonomous clinical decisions, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations are introduced by GA finalization.
