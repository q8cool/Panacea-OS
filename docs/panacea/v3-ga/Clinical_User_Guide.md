# Version 3.0 Clinical User Guide

## Clinical Safety Position

Panacea OS Enterprise Version 3.0 supports governed clinical-adjacent enterprise workflows. It does not replace clinicians and does not perform autonomous diagnosis or treatment.

## Human Oversight

Clinical users remain the final decision makers. AI, governance, privacy, compliance, and operational outputs are advisory, traceable, and subject to local policy.

## Safety Controls

- Recommendation traceability.
- Human approval workflows.
- Clinical and patient safety workflow auditability.
- Medication and allergy safety validation requirements.
- Emergency, ICU, and surgery workflow safety validation.

## Incident Handling

Clinical safety concerns must be recorded through patient safety, compliance, audit, legal governance, or AI incident workflows as appropriate.
