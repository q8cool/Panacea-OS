## Future Version

| Feature | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Autonomous treatment execution | Conflicts with the human-governed safety posture and requires future legal, clinical, and regulatory maturity. |
| Fully self-modifying production AI | Conflicts with governed learning and approved promotion requirements. |
| Global patient identity authority replacement | Requires national-level mandates beyond product scope. |

## Deferred

| Feature | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Fully autonomous procurement execution | Keep Version 4 focused on governed recommendations and approved workflows. |
| Patient-facing autonomous triage | Requires additional safety, jurisdiction, liability, and clinical governance evidence. |
| Open marketplace publishing without certification | Deferred to protect trust, safety, privacy, and enterprise compliance. |

## Rejected

| Feature | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Autonomous diagnosis | Rejected because clinicians remain the final decision makers. |
| Autonomous prescribing | Rejected because treatment decisions require licensed human approval. |
| Unsanctioned cross-border data sharing | Rejected because privacy, consent, trust, and residency controls are mandatory. |
| Unapproved AI production promotion | Rejected because AI governance requires authorized approval. |
