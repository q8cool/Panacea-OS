# Sprint 86 Backlog — Advanced Clinical Intelligence Governance

## Purpose

Extend Version 4.0 clinical intelligence governance with stronger recommendation review, evidence traceability, clinical safety cases, and human approval workflows.

## Proposed Scope

- Clinical intelligence governance expansion.
- Evidence ledger governance.
- Safety case registry for clinical recommendations.
- Multidisciplinary review workflow.
- Recommendation traceability review.
- AI assurance revalidation linkage.

## Out Of Scope

- Autonomous diagnosis.
- Autonomous treatment.
- Autonomous prescribing.
- Clinician approval bypass.
- Unapproved production AI promotion.

## Dependencies

- Sprint 84 Autonomous Healthcare Intelligence Foundation.
- Sprint 85 Real-Time Global Healthcare Command Intelligence Platform.
- Clinical Intelligence Platform.
- AI Assurance Platform.
- Global Knowledge Network.
- Audit Service.
- Notification Service.

## Validation Requirements

- Contract tests.
- Integration tests.
- Human approval enforcement tests.
- Clinical safety validation tests.
- Evidence traceability validation tests.
- Multi-country policy validation tests.
